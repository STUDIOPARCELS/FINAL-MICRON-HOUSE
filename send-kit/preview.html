How to send it

  1. Put the invitation online, once

    Upload the three files in send-kit/web/4tesla (index.html, terms.pdf, preview.jpg) so they live at warmspringshouse.com/4tesla. The page stays out of search engines.

  2. Email your introduction

    Paste the subject and text. Drag the GIF into the body, select it, press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) and paste the invitation link. Attach the term sheet PDF. Send it to yourself first and open it on your phone.

  3. Direct messages on X or Instagram

    From your phone, send Warm Springs House - Prepared for Tesla.mp4 from your camera roll, then paste the message.

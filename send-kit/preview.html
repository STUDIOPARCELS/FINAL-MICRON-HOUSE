Tesla Send Kit

How the invitation reaches Tesla: one email, direct messages on X or Instagram, the invitation online and the video. Every file is in the send-kit folder on your branch.

How Tesla experiences it

Every screen below is the live site on a phone. Swipe sideways through each path.

By email

  1. 1It arrives

    From Lisa Wood, lisa@lisawoodstudio.com. Your name, the subject and the first line are all they see before opening. No attachment, nothing hidden, no tracking.

  2. 2They open the email

    A short note, then the card: the seal opens to Prepared for Tesla and loops (8 seconds). It plays inside the email.

  3. 3They tap the card

    warmspringshouse.com/4tesla?card=1 opens. They have just watched the seal open, so the page skips it and Card 1 drops in.

  4. 4Card 1 plays

    The house, Warm Springs House, then the words line by line. Full screen on a phone, a framed card on a computer.

  5. 5The cards move on by themselves

    Each card plays once, holds long enough to read, then the next slides up over it, until they touch the screen. After that they swipe at their own pace.

  6. 6Card 6: see it in person

    Address, your phone, email and site, all tappable. Presented by Lisa Wood Studio links to your studio.

  7. 7The terms, if linked

    A one-page web page at warmspringshouse.com/4tesla/terms, reached only from a link in your message. Out of search engines.

By X or Instagram message

  1. 1The video arrives

    63 seconds, vertical, no sound: the whole invitation, opening on the sealed card.

  2. 2They tap the link

    warmspringshouse.com/4tesla opens on the sealed card, with the house on view under the seal.

  3. 3The seal opens

    Prepared for Tesla types in, then the cards follow as above.

How to send it

  1. Put the invitation online, once

    Already live at warmspringshouse.com/4tesla, with the terms at warmspringshouse.com/4tesla/terms. The page stays out of search engines.

  2. Email

    Open a new message from lisa@lisawoodstudio.com. Press Copy subject and paste it into the subject, then Copy email and paste it into the body: the text, the animated card (linked to the invitation), the links and your signature all come across. Change [First name], add their address and send. No attachment. Send one to yourself first and open it on your phone.

  3. Direct messages on X or Instagram

    From your phone, send Warm Springs House - Prepared for Tesla.mp4 from your camera roll, then paste the message.

Policy

Two legislative packages prepared for the Idaho Legislature and Boise City Council. Autonomous driving systems at the state level, robot-enabled building operations at the municipal level.

Brief A · State Legislature

Idaho Automated Driving Systems and Driverless Passenger Service Act

New Chapter 38, Title 49, Idaho Code · March 2026
Modeled on Utah's HB 101 (House 70–0, Senate 23–0). Defines automated driving systems using SAE J3016, creates a driverless passenger-service category, and establishes fleet operator accountability. Zero new agencies, zero new taxes, zero new fees.

Brief B · City Council

Boise Robot-Enabled Operations Pilot Ordinance

State PDD Amendments + City Pilot · March 2026
Extends Boise AI governance (Regulation 4.30q) to physical robotic systems. Three-year sunset, voluntary opt-in, administered through existing PDS and Fire Department infrastructure. Zero new agencies, zero new taxes, zero new commissions.

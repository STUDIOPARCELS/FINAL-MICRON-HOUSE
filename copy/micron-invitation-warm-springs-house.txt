CARD 3 — SECURITY + SERVICE + MOBILITY  [conceptual: Optimus inside, Cybercab outside on private grounds]

Security + Service + Mobility

WARM SPRINGS HOUSE is a residential test kitchen. Optimus is tested on security rounds and household service; Cybercab, on mobility. One or two Tesla engineers live and work on site.

Tesla sets the scope, safety and operating requirements for its technology and directs its own people and data. Micron and Tesla set timing and visibility. The residency can be entirely private.

Participation by Micron, Tesla and Boise institutions remains to be confirmed, as do technology availability, operating approvals and program dates.

CARD 4 — MICRON'S CORPORATE ASSET  [actual living, dining + gathering spaces]

Exclusive Corporate Use

A long-term lease reserves WARM SPRINGS HOUSE for Micron's corporate use. It keeps its name until a corporate partner commits.

Executive stays. Private meetings + dinners. Company hospitality. Recruiting + relocation.

Micron directs its use, the residency included: who stays, who visits and what is shared.


CARD 5 — MICRON'S LEADERSHIP OPPORTUNITY  [the house as a setting for conversation]

A Leadership Opportunity

Autonomous systems run on memory.

At the house, Micron convenes universities, hospitals, government and community leaders around autonomy in daily life and what it means for Boise's workforce, health care and public services.

Early 2027 is the envisioned window for these conversations. What Boise learns can inform other cities.

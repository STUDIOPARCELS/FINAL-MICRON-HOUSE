CARD 2 — AUTUMN HOUSE REVEAL

WARM SPRINGS HOUSE
The autonomous corporate residence + entertainment hub.

A 1906 home of 3,374 square feet at 1020 East Warm Springs Avenue, on a National Register corridor five minutes from the Capitol and downtown.

Boise has a habit of practical invention. In 1892, the C.W. Moore mansion just down the avenue became one of the first homes in America heated by geothermal water. That water still warms this house's radiators today.

Available now. Ready to host.


CARD 3 — THE TECHNOLOGY + PROOF OF CONCEPT

A Residential Test Kitchen

Tesla is invited to bring Optimus + Cybercab to the house, with one or two Tesla engineers living and working on site.

Optimus provides security and service: perimeter checks, household tasks, support for guests. Cybercab provides mobility: arrivals, departures and transfers across Boise. Real stays, real dinners, a real home: the proof of concept.

Tesla sets technical scope and operating requirements. Participating teams set timing and visibility, including an entirely private residency.

The house is ready now; Tesla's participation, hardware availability and program dates remain to be confirmed.

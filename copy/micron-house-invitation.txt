MICRON HOUSE
BOISE, IDAHO

A new kind of corporate asset: a private residence, a hospitality hub and a proof of concept for autonomous service.

WARM SPRINGS HOUSE is offered to Micron on a long-term lease for exclusive corporate use, and through that partnership becomes MICRON HOUSE. Executive stays, private meetings, dinners, company hospitality, recruiting and relocation visits, all at Micron's direction.

Available now. Ready to host.

Within that setting, Tesla is invited to bring Optimus for security and service and Cybercab for mobility, with one or two Tesla engineers living and working on site: a residential test kitchen, hosted by Micron.
